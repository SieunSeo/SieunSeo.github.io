About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at Ewha Womans University. My work spans mathematical analysis of lattice-based cryptographic algorithms, CKKS bootstrapping and key switching, and C++/CUDA implementation for efficient encrypted AI inference.

My research goal is to bridge the gap between cryptographic theory and practical, high-performance privacy-preserving AI systems.
