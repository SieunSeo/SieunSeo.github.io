ABOUT ME

Advancing Privacy-Preserving AI
through Efficient Encrypted Inference.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at Ewha Womans University, specializing in homomorphic encryption and privacy-preserving AI.

My research focuses on enabling efficient and practical privacy-preserving inference for Transformers and large language models (LLMs). I work on optimizing encrypted computation through algorithmic design, mathematical analysis, and high-performance C++/CUDA implementations, bridging the gap between cryptographic theory and real-world AI applications.
